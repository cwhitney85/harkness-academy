Conversational Basics: Getting By in Spanish

Five lessons · about 3 hours · 101 level
Your goals mention reading Borges in the original. That sits outside Conversational Basics — it is closer to Literary Reading. This syllabus covers the conversational ground you asked for; the reading goal is not addressed here.

Five lessons that take you from a standing start to holding up your end of a short exchange — greeting someone, saying how and where you are, asking for what you need, handling numbers and prices, and getting directions when you are lost. Everything stays in the present tense; past and future are the next course's job.

We will spend more time than usual on ser and estar, because you said in the interview that you have bounced off them twice before.
