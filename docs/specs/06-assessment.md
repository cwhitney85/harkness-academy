# Spec 6 — Assessment

*Provides: quiz service (6.1) — the fixed contract parallel lanes build against. Consumes: 2, 3, 4 (syllabus concepts), 5 (SUMMARY docs, completion pipeline).*

**Decisions that bind this spec** ([ledger](../02-design.md), index at its head): [D12](../02-design.md) 6.1 owns the service, package 5 landed the tables · [D18](../02-design.md) what the quiz service owns at each grain · [D19](../02-design.md) homework reaches WK as its own prompt variable · [D20](../02-design.md) an exam measures, it does not teach · [D25](../02-design.md) opening an exam is queued work · [D26](../02-design.md) a half-finished exam is a saved answer sheet · [D32](../02-design.md) a worksheet discloses as it goes; the exam does not · [D33](../02-design.md) the exam screen's states are the server's · [D34](../02-design.md) `final_exam_concepts` is emphasis, not scope · [D40](../02-design.md) the writer deals the key a position, and there are four options to deal. Postmortem: [T5](../lessons.md).

- **6.1 MUST** — Polymorphic `quizzes` + `quiz_attempts` serving all four types (checkpoint / homework / final / refresher) through one service. *Done: four types create rows via one service.*
- **6.2 MUST** — Homework: quiz + suggested reading on lesson completion (rides 5.4.1); result feeds next WK update. *Done: homework attempt visible in WK diff.*
- **6.3 MUST** — Final exam: covers every lesson the syllabus teaches, gates `completed`. *Done: pass flips state; fail records attempt, course stays `active`.*
  - **6.3.1 MUST** — Coverage is per lesson, not per concept. Every lesson in the syllabus is reachable through at least one question; no concept is individually required. A tag resolves to its lesson through `SyllabusConcepts`, which already holds slug → lesson positions. Replaces the rule that every slug in `final_exam_concepts` had to be some question's tag, which could not be satisfied at course size ([T5](../lessons.md)). *Done: an exam whose questions touch all 8 of course 1's lessons passes; one constructed to skip lesson 3 is refused and says which lesson; one that omits 30 individual concepts but touches every lesson passes.*
  - **6.3.2 MUST** — The exam asks two questions per lesson. `QuizLength::exam()` takes the lesson count from the caller, which already loads the syllabus; `homework()` and `refresher()` are untouched. Depth parameters allow 5–10 lessons, so the exam runs 10–20 questions and `pass_mark: 0.7` resolves to 70–75% — no wider than the 70–78% the fixed 8–12 range produced (ledger [D20](../02-design.md) rider 3, amended). The prompt states **both** the computed total and the per-lesson rule — `question_count` is a prompt variable rather than prose, because the model doing the arithmetic itself invites a rejected reply at `top`-tier prices, and because "student picks 1–5 per lesson" (v1.1) is then a different number rather than an edited prompt. *Done: a 5-lesson course generates 10 questions and a 10-lesson course generates 20; the rendered prompt states that same number and the per-lesson rule; the syllabus screen shows the number the exam then asks; the guard holding prompt and schema together fails if any of the three disagree.*
  - **6.3.3 MUST** — A question's `concept_tag` resolves against every concept the lesson sections declare (`SyllabusConcepts::declared()`), not against `final_exam_concepts`. Without this, a question about a real lesson-4 concept outside the declared exam list is stripped of its tag and its lesson counts as uncovered, so 6.3.1 could not be satisfied. *Done: a question tagged with a declared concept outside `final_exam_concepts` keeps its tag and counts toward its lesson; a question tagged with an invented slug still has its tag nulled.*
  - **6.3.4 SHOULD** — The answer sheet: a student's selections are saved as they are made, and grading still happens once, at hand-in. One row per (exam, student, sitting) holding question id → option id, replaced on each save, holding **selections only and no verdicts** — which is what keeps [D20](../02-design.md)'s no-disclosure rule intact. `QuizGrader` remains the sole writer of `quiz_attempts` and `correct` stays `NOT NULL`. The sheet is kept when the sitting is handed in, and a re-sit opens a new, empty one ([16.2.12](16-catalog-removal-ownership.md), amended 2026-09-23 by [D69](../02-design.md)). Saving on each selection MUST; debouncing MAY. Ejectable — the exam is sittable without it, and it is the only item in 6.3.* that can be dropped without leaving the course broken. *Done: answer six of sixteen, reload, the six ticks are still there; hand in, reload, the result renders and the sheet no longer opens as a draft; fail, re-sit, the book opens with nothing ticked; one student's sheet is never visible to another.*
  - **6.3.5 MUST** — A regression guard at realistic size. Every fixture in the suite declares three exam concepts, which is small enough to hide a rule that only binds at course size ([T5](../lessons.md)). *Done: a test builds a syllabus of at least 8 lessons and 40+ declared concepts and asserts an exam generates; deleting 6.3.1's per-lesson rule makes a test fail rather than only production.*
- **6.4 MUST** — Refresher quizzes, subtopic + lesson grain, from WK + SUMMARY docs. *Done: lesson refresher questions traceable to its summary.*
- **6.5 SHOULD** — Module-grain quiz via 3.3 pointer-walk. *Done: 2-subtopic module quiz draws from both WKs.*

## Dogfood repair (2026-09-07)

- **6.6 MUST** — The correct option's position is distributed, not left to the model. `Quizzes::create()` deals each question's key a position as the quiz is written — least-used-first within the document, ties broken at random, only the key moving ([D40](../02-design.md)) — because it is the single writer all four kinds reach. Before it did, options rendered in the order the model emitted them and observed checkpoints clustered on A and B. *Done: across 40 generated questions no option position holds the correct answer more than 40% of the time; a test asserts the distribution over a fixed set.*
  - **6.6.1 SHOULD** — Four options, not three or four. `MIN_OPTIONS` and `MAX_OPTIONS` are both 4, so 6.6 always has a D to deal; the four prompts that write questions say four, and say that an option must stand alone — the application decides where each appears, so "all of the above" is wrong by the time the student reads it. *Done: the constants express the intended shape; a D position exists to be chosen.*

## Acceptance test

*The journey this package's items compose into. The per-item done-conditions each prove one item; this proves they work together (ledger [D29](../02-design.md)). It is runnable without opening another spec.*

On a course whose lessons are all complete:

1. Open the last lesson's homework. Answer one question: its explanation and its key appear only then, and the page held neither before. That attempt is visible in the next working-knowledge update.
2. Open the final exam. The request is queued and the screen waits; the paper that arrives asks two questions per lesson — ten for a five-lesson course, twenty for a ten-lesson one — and every lesson in the syllabus is reachable through at least one question, including lessons whose concepts are nowhere in `final_exam_concepts`. Nothing on the page or in its payload discloses an answer.
3. Answer six of the questions and reload. The six ticks are still there and no verdict is. Another student cannot see that sheet.
4. Hand in a failing paper. The sitting is recorded, the course stays active, no draft sheet remains, and re-sitting opens the same exam with nothing ticked.
5. Re-sit and pass at 70%. The course is stamped completed.
6. Take a refresher at each of the three grains. The lesson grain's questions are traceable to that lesson's summary; the subtopic grain draws on its working knowledge; the module grain walks the rollup and draws on at least two subtopics.
7. Across the questions generated in this run, the correct answer is not clustered on any one option position, and four options are the norm.
